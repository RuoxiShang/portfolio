Hi I'm Anna, a PhD candidate at the [University of Washington](https://www.hcde.washington.edu/).

My work focuses on evaluating and steering LLM behavior for trust, alignment, and reliability. I develop theory-grounded benchmarks and evaluation pipeline to measure model risks in realistic settings, and I design human-in-the-loop experiments to audit the downstream impact of AI agents. I have published 10+ peer-reviewed papers (CHI, EMNLP, FAccT, AIES) and contributed to foundational research at Microsoft Research and Oumi AI.
