Hi, I'm Anna, a PhD candidate at the [University of Washington](https://www.hcde.washington.edu/). Previously, I studied Applied Mathematics and Statistics at [UC Berkeley](https://www.berkeley.edu/).

My work evaluates and audits AI systems in settings where aggregate binary metrics are not enough. Throughout my PhD research, I have used benchmark design, synthetic scenario generation, rubric-based LLM judging, human-subjects studies, and hypothesis-driven analysis to assess AI systems. My work spans both automated evaluation pipelines and the design of processes that help human experts provide more reliable verification and feedback.

I am generally interested in ways to make AI systems trustworthy, aligned with human values, and useful.
